How can we help?

Answers to common questions are below. If yours isn't here, get in touch and we'll help.

Questions

I bought Cozy+ but it's locked on my new iPhone.

Open Cozy Sounds, tap Cozy+ at the top, then tap Restore under the button. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can my family use my Cozy+ purchase?

Yes. Cozy+ supports Family Sharing, so everyone in your Family Sharing group gets it at no extra cost.

Is Cozy+ a subscription?

No. It's a single one-time purchase. There are no renewals and nothing to cancel.

Does the sound keep playing when I lock my iPhone?

Yes. Cozy Sounds keeps playing in the background and with the screen locked. You can pause it from the Lock Screen, Control Center or the Dynamic Island.

How does the sleep timer work?

Open the player by tapping the bar at the bottom of the screen. Choose 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, or drag the slider for any time between 5 minutes and 4 hours. The sound fades out gently over the last 30 seconds.

How do I add a widget?

Widgets and the Control Center control are part of Cozy+. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Cozy Sounds. Touch and hold a widget and choose Edit Widget to pick which moods or sounds it plays.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All sounds are included in the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore Cozy+.
