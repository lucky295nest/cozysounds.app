Where the sounds come from

Every recording in Cozy Sounds comes from Freesound.org and is released under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) public domain dedication, which allows commercial use without permission or attribution. We list them anyway, with thanks to the recordists. Each link opens the original recording and shows its license.

The recordings were trimmed into seamless loops and level-matched. White, pink and brown noise are generated by the app itself.
