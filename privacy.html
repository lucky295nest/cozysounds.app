Privacy Policy

Your nights stay yours.

Effective October 16, 2026

Cozy Sounds does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

The mixes you save, your chosen theme and what's currently playing are stored only on your device. Some of this is kept in storage shared between the app and its own widgets, so your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can show your mixes. None of it is sent to us or anyone else, and deleting the app removes it.

All sounds are included in the app, so playing them doesn't need an internet connection.

Purchases

Cozy+ is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether Cozy+ is unlocked on your Apple Account. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Cozy Sounds is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
